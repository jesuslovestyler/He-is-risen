# He Is Risen

A gentle, invitational case for the resurrection of Jesus — for skeptics and seekers asking the honest question: did it really happen?

Built around five historical considerations:

- **He died** — the crucifixion, granted even by skeptical scholars
- **The tomb was empty** — the first witnesses were women, a detail no invented story would choose
- **The witnesses were transformed** — frightened deserters became witnesses willing to die for what they claimed to have seen
- **The creed is early** — the 1 Corinthians 15 confession dates within a few years of the event and names living witnesses
- **Skeptics were converted** — James, Paul

Warm and honest, never combative — it invites rather than corners, and points the reader toward the Gospel presentation for what the resurrection means personally.

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible, a public-domain translation.

Built with Muse artifacts.
